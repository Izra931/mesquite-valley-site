Pieces we've made

These are designs we build often. Each one is made by hand, so yours will be sized for your room and cut from its own piece of wood. Click a photo to see it larger, and use the button under any design to ask us about it.

Mesquite and wrought iron coffee table

Our signature table. A solid mesquite frame holds a custom glass top, so you can see the points of the iron base beneath it. The base is forged by an award-winning blacksmith, with hand-hammered copper accents.

Ask about: length and height, glass or solid wood top, matching end tables.

Burl table with turquoise

Cut from a solid 3-inch burl slab. Genuine Arizona turquoise fills the natural openings in the wood, sealed under clear resin so the swirling grain shows through. It sits on a hand-forged iron base with solid brass accents.

No two burls are alike. We'll find one that suits you.

Fireplace mantels

Mantels in mesquite, pecan, cedar and walnut, rustic or refined. We choose the exact piece for your fireplace from over a hundred we keep on hand, so the grain and shape fit the room.

Ask about: length, depth, hand-hewn or smooth finish, mounting.

Countertops and island tops

Kitchen counters and island tops in mesquite, pecan and Texas walnut, built to your measurements. We can seal them against water for life or give them a hand-rubbed oil finish.

Options include sink cutouts, custom edges, matching cutting boards, and installation.

Bar tops

Long, solid bar tops for homes, ranches and lodges. This 10-foot mesquite bar was made for a local hunting lodge.

Ask about: length, edge style, and gloss or satin finish.

Keepsake boxes

Solid mesquite boxes with genuine USA turquoise inlay, about 8 by 6 inches. A place for jewelry, photos and small treasures, and a good gift for someone who has everything.

Also available without inlay, and in a larger 10-inch size.
